Violations in this window

Counting the journal…

The $50 line is three rows. A recorded plan over $50 is a planned-risk violation and counts against compliance. A loss over $50 inside a plan of $50 or less is slippage: it is shown here and does not flip the compliance flag. With no planned risk on the row, a loss over $50 stays the historical proxy and still counts. Stick to the plan is the journal tag “broke plan”. It is not one of the thirteen lines, and it still counts. A trade can trip more than one check, so the dollars are not a split of net P&L.

Rule Flagged Gross P&L

Going forward

Every new trade should carry a real checklist. Blank cells stay inferred, and a blank planned-risk cell is never filled in from a guess.

  • Planned stop, in stop.
  • Planned risk, in planned_risk. Leave it blank only if stop, entry, and share size are all numbers: the site then uses |entry − stop| × size.
  • idea_id, shared by the starter, the adds, the stop-outs, and the reentries. Example: 2026-11-17-ABCD-1.
  • Setup valid, setup_valid as Y or N.
  • size_reason: planned, A+ setup, emotional, or other.
  • Each rule as Y or N: confirmation, risk $50, no revenge, no averaging, three stops, stop and size, plan.

This history is an upper bound

Historical compliance is derived from journal notes, mistakes tags, and P&L. It is not a checklist he filled out when he placed the trade. A note that says nothing looks like a clean trade, so the percentage is likely higher than a real-time card would have been. Rows that include the optional Y/N columns are different: a filled cell replaces the inferred flag for that one rule. Blank cells on the same row still use the inference.

Gross dollars, as written in the journal. Not a brokerage statement.
